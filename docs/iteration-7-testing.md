# Iteration 7: what to check on the Switch

Fully close Isaac, install, then start the game. This build also contains the
iteration 6 balance changes: if they were not installed before (the installer
says so), finish or abandon your current run and start a new one.

1. **Main menu**: open **Mods** (game menu), press **X**. The paper menu
   opens on the Mods page. Switch a mod off with **A**, open **Menu** with
   **R**, press **B** to close.
2. **Restart the game** (fully close and reopen): open the menu again: the mod
   you switched off is still off. Switch it back on.
3. **Pause in a run** and look for "Y Mods" in the bottom-right. Press **Y**:
   - **Items**: browse with the D-pad, **ZL/ZR** a page at a time, **X** for
     the filter card. Press **A** on an item, choose *Place on a pedestal*,
     close the menu with **+**, unpause: the item is next to Isaac.
   - Try *Give to Isaac* on a card or pill and *Place as a shop item* on a
     collectible (check the price looks normal).
   - **Run**: seed, floor and stats match the game's own.
   - **Tools**: try *+10 coins* and *Reveal map*.
   - **B** to close: the pause screen is still there and did not react.
4. **Hold both sticks + D-pad Down** for 2 seconds in a cleared room: the menu
   opens without pausing. **Menu → Shortcut hold → 0.5 s**, close, try again.
5. **Mods → Hit Counter → Y**: move it, change size, close: the counter stays
   where you put it after restarting the game.
6. **Mods → Time Machine → Y**: lower the top speed and use a slot machine.
7. Look for anything drawn wrong: missing pictures, text in the wrong colour,
   the left/right page arrows, button pictures.

If something is wrong, note what you did and what you saw. The installer keeps
the previous module and lists how to undo everything in the deployment
record's `ROLLBACK.md`. The settings file can stay; older builds ignore it.
